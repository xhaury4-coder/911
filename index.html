01 — Who is Vishayकानपुर

Kanpur raised him. The mic chose him.

Vishay comes straight from the heart of Kanpur — a city of factories, late-night chai, and stories that never sleep. Together with Rishit as The 9eleven, the bars carry the weight of real streets and the hunger of every young dreamer who ever picked up a pen.

No industry polish. No borrowed accent. Just pure flow, sharp wordplay, and the kind of honesty that hits harder than any beat. When they drop, the lanes listen.
